*Set July 2026. Finish line: age ~70. Direction fixed, route flexible.*

1. **EECS**
2. **Mechanical Engineering**
3. **Aerospace Engineering**

Plus olympiad-level knowledge (knowledge, not medals) across: math, physics, chemistry, IOI/informatics, linguistics, philosophy, research olympiads, and robotics olympiads.

## Method: Spiral Curriculum with Deep Cycles

- **Cycle length:** 3–4 months on ONE primary domain, 5–6 hours daily.
- **Maintenance mode:** the other domains get 45 minutes TOTAL per day — active recall only (one hard olympiad problem, one Arabic translation, one paper). No passive note-reading.
- **The spiral:** after each deep cycle, rotate. Over the decades, each domain gets many intense deep cycles, building interconnected schema instead of keeping everything at sophomore level.
- Applied projects on everything learned, using AUC facilities and labs + professors' mentorship.

## Guardrails (accepted)

- At every cycle rotation, the map may be revised **without it counting as failure**. The plan is a compass, not a cage.
- **Rest is a first-class scheduled item.** Assume ~80% uptime, not 100% — a plan that assumes perfect uptime for 48 years breaks its operator; one with fatigue margins finishes.
- **Max 1–2 time-management books, then act.** The first completed cycle is the real teacher.
- Regularly revisit mastered material at the lowest sustainable pace so it never goes volatile.
